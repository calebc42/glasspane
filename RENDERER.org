#+title: Glasspane Renderer Contract

* Design choice

Glasspane is a Material 3 application built on the Jetpacs Compose foundation.
That choice belongs to Glasspane; it is not an implicit property of Jetpacs or
of EBP.

Both branches of =glasspane-register= declare
=:requires-extensions '("jetpacs.material3")=. On a live Companion, Jetpacs
must observe that identifier in the app target's EBP 3 =extensions= profile
before entering or building Glasspane. If it is absent, the Apps surface shows
the missing requirement and leaves Glasspane state and builders untouched.

Material-only wire nodes are also dual-gated by their namespaced node type and
the same extension. Their schema lives in Jetpacs' renderer manifest, not in
EBP. Glasspane may use those nodes because it makes the app-level requirement
explicit. Foundation modules must not infer the requirement from a node-name
prefix or import Material on Glasspane's behalf.

* Verification

The focused Glasspane ERT suite asserts the registry declaration. The Jetpacs
app and floor suites separately prove the unavailable-app flow and the
node-plus-extension sender gate. Android renderer profiles are derived from
installed renderer contributions rather than a second handwritten welcome
list.
